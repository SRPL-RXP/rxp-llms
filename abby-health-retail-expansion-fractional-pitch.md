## Why fractional, and why now

The role as written is a full-time hire. I want to be direct about why I think a fractional engagement serves Abby better at this stage.

You are pre-first-site. The most expensive decisions in the entire rollout (which centres, which lease terms, what the opening model costs to run) are made before there is any revenue to support a full-time executive package plus equity. A fractional engagement gives you the senior judgement exactly when the stakes are highest and the fixed cost is hardest to justify.

The work is front-loaded and then it repeats. Getting the first site from confirmation to opening day is intensive and hands-on. Once the playbook exists, each subsequent opening is a known quantity that a small ops team executes against a template. Fractional matches the shape of the work: heavy through the pilot, structured and delegated through the rollout.

You keep optionality. If, after the pilot and playbook, you want to convert the engagement into a permanent hire (me or someone I help you recruit), you do that from a position of knowledge, with a proven model and a documented operation, rather than betting a full-time package on an unproven format.

Fractional does not mean part-attention. Through the pilot I operate as your Head of Retail Expansion in every practical sense: I own the site, the lease, the fit-out, the compliance, and the opening. The difference is structure and cost, not commitment.

## How the engagement would work

A phased structure that mirrors Abby's own plan.

### Phase 1: Pilot launch
I own the first kiosk from site confirmation to opening day: council compliance, supplier setup, the daily operations manual, staff induction, uniforms, equipment, and opening stock, working alongside your internal teams on fit-out and staffing. Deliverable: one Abby Clinic location, operationally ready and open.

### Phase 2: Playbook
As the pilot runs, I document everything: what works, what does not, and what each location needs to open successfully. Deliverable: a clear, repeatable opening model that becomes the foundation of the national rollout.

### Phase 3: National rollout
Site selection, lease negotiation, fit-out project management, local hiring, and operational setup across additional locations, including managing sites opening simultaneously or in rapid succession. I help you build and hire the small ops team (local ops managers and clinic coordinators) that runs the network, and I can coordinate property, fit-out, finance, and recruitment specialists through Rx Property's existing affiliate network where that is faster than building it in-house.

### Phase 4: Ongoing operations
Abby Clinic P&L reporting and daily performance metrics, shopping centre operator relationships, retail and health and safety compliance across the network, and escalation of underperforming sites to leadership.

Time commitment scales by phase: heaviest through the pilot, then structured and increasingly delegated as the playbook and ops team mature. Commercial terms (a retainer through the pilot, and a rollout structure with upside as the network grows) I would shape with you once we have talked about scope and pace.
