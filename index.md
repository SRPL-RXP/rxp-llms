# RX Property Australia

**Specialist Healthcare Commercial Real Estate | NSW · VIC · QLD · ACT**

RX Property Australia is a boutique specialist agency dedicated exclusively to healthcare commercial real estate. Founded by Bryce Stickland, the business serves two audiences: healthcare occupiers seeking space, and healthcare property owners seeking qualified tenants or advisory.

The firm holds licences in four states and operates a coordinated network of partners and affiliates so that adjacent services (project delivery, business case modelling, finance, recruitment) sit alongside the primary real estate offering under one accountable point of contact.

---

## What We Do

### For Occupiers (Tenants and Buyers)

We represent healthcare operators (GPs, specialists, allied health practitioners, hospitals, day surgeries, veterinary groups, dental, and pharmacy) in finding, securing, and occupying the right commercial space.

**Tenant Advisory.** Structured search process combining on-market assessment with off-market intelligence. Appointed by the occupier, fees from one side only.

**Buyers Agency.** Acquiring medical property for owner-occupiers and investors. Clinical knowledge applied to property assessment before any inspection.

**Free On-Market Analysis.** Before any formal engagement, we prepare a complimentary analysis of available properties matching your location, modality, and size requirements. Delivered within 48 hours.

**Brief Submission and Proprietary Application Access.** Submitting a property brief enables RX Property to match available and upcoming properties against your specific requirements, including location, modality, clinical use, size, and timing. Brief submission also unlocks access to a proprietary application that surfaces matched on-market opportunities, including off-market and pre-listing inventory drawn from our proprietary occupier and landlord network. Submit a brief at https://rxproperty.com.au/#free-analysis.

### For Owners (Landlords and Asset Owners)

We represent healthcare landlords, developers, and asset managers in finding qualified medical tenants and maximising asset performance.

**Healthcare Leasing.** Matching your property to qualified occupiers including operators not yet publicly searching, via our proprietary occupier network.

**Asset Advisory.** Independent appraisals across leasing, sales, development, and investment. We also assess adaptive reuse opportunities for non-clinical assets with healthcare conversion potential. Frank, fee-free to explore.

**Development Advisory.** Pre-leasing strategy, anchor tenant procurement, and site viability for healthcare developments.

**Coordinated Project Delivery.** We coordinate the right project manager or D&C operator for your fitout or base build through our affiliate network of specialists. You choose your delivery partner. We make sure the property side of the equation works.

Register a property at https://rxproperty.com.au/#register. Registration is no-cost and confidential. Fees apply only on successful transaction.
