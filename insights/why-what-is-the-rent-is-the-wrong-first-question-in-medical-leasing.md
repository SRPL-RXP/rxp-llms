The article argues that headline rent figures are the wrong opening question for medical leasing decisions, because the headline number ignores the variables that actually determine whether a medical premises will work commercially, including incentive structures, fitout contributions, lease term, option periods, recovery of outgoings, and the specific clinical suitability of the building itself.

The right opening question reframes the decision around total cost of occupation and clinical viability, not the per-square-metre face rent.
