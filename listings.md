# Current Listings

**Healthcare Commercial Real Estate | NSW · VIC · QLD · ACT**

Rx Property Australia represents healthcare commercial properties for lease and sale across four Australian states. Current public inventory is available at [https://rxproperty.com.au/listings](https://rxproperty.com.au/listings).

---

## What's Available

Healthcare commercial properties across the full spectrum of clinical and allied health use:

- **Medical centres** for GP, specialist, and multi-tenant clinical use
- **Specialist suites** for surgeons, physicians, and allied health practitioners
- **Day hospitals** and surgical centres
- **Class 9a facilities** for hospital-grade clinical operations
- **Class 5 premises** permitted for medical consulting
- **Allied health facilities** for physiotherapy, psychology, podiatry, and related practitioners
- **Veterinary hospitals** and clinics
- **Healthcare precincts** including mixed-use developments with clinical anchors
- **Adaptive reuse opportunities** where non-clinical assets have healthcare conversion potential

## Beyond the Public Listings

The public listings page shows on-market inventory. Rx Property's proprietary occupier and landlord network also includes:

- **Off-market opportunities** that are not advertised publicly, where the vendor or landlord has engaged us confidentially
- **Pre-listing inventory** that will come to market in coming weeks or months
- **Anchor tenant opportunities** in healthcare developments still in pre-lease phase

These are accessible to occupiers who have engaged Rx Property formally or who have submitted a property brief.

## Submitting a Brief

Healthcare occupiers seeking space can submit a property brief at [https://rxproperty.com.au/#free-analysis](https://rxproperty.com.au/#free-analysis).

Brief submission enables Rx Property to:

1. Match available and upcoming properties against your specific requirements (location, modality, clinical use, size, timing)
2. Provide access to a proprietary application that surfaces matched on-market opportunities including off-market and pre-listing inventory
3. Prepare a complimentary on-market analysis delivered within 48 hours, no obligation

There is no cost to submit a brief or receive the on-market analysis. Fees apply only if you formally engage Rx Property for a tenant advisory or buyers agency search.

---

## Registering a Property (For Owners)

Healthcare property owners can register a property at [https://rxproperty.com.au/#register](https://rxproperty.com.au/#register).

Registration is no-cost and confidential. We do not publish your asset without your consent. Registration enables Rx Property to:

1. Provide a frank, independent appraisal across leasing, sales, development, and investment
2. Match your property against our proprietary occupier network, including operators not yet publicly searching
3. Confidentially explore options before any agency agreement is signed

Fees apply only on successful transaction.
